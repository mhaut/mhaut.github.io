The IEEE JSTARS special issue **AI-Driven Hyperspectral Remote Sensing: From Algorithms to Applications** opened for submissions, with Juan M. Haut among its Guest Editors. The [official call for papers](https://www.grss-ieee.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/JSTARS_CfP_Template_-Revised-Version_0230_2.pdf) lists the submission period as 1 January–30 June 2026.
