**OASIS**, a research project on sustainable AI for hyperspectral remote sensing, began on 1 September 2024. Project PID2023-149880NA-I00 is funded by MICIU/AEI and FEDER, EU, and runs until 31 December 2027, as detailed in the [University of Extremadura's project documentation](https://rrhhinvestigacion.unex.es/wp-content/uploads/sites/58/2025/03/IN000482_C.pdf).
