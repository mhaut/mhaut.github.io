**RAT_EOS_PC** began on 1 September 2023, developing early-warning networks for climate risks using Earth observation. The project is co-funded by the EU through Interreg Spain–Portugal (POCTEP) and runs until 31 December 2026. See the [project description](https://www.estrategiaagros.es/transferencia/proyectos/rat_eos_pc).
