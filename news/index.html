Research, academic appointments and student achievements, newest first.

Oct 19, 2026 As of October 2026, Juan M. Haut serves as an Editorial Board Member of Scientific Reports.
Oct 19, 2026 In 2026, Juan M. Haut joined Remote Sensing as an Associate Editor. He has also served as Guest Editor of multiple special issues.
May 23, 2026 Appointment as Tenured Associate Professor (Profesor Titular de Universidad) at the University of Extremadura, in the area of Computer Architecture and Technology. The resolution of 12 May 2026 was published in the BOE on 23 May 2026.
Jan 01, 2026 The IEEE JSTARS special issue AI-Driven Hyperspectral Remote Sensing: From Algorithms to Applications opened for submissions, with Juan M. Haut among its Guest Editors. The official call for papers lists the submission period as 1 January–30 June 2026.
Nov 07, 2025 Zhaoyue Wu received an Outstanding Doctoral Award in the technical field at the University of Extremadura. The recognition was announced during the School of Doctoral Studies celebration.
Jul 21, 2025 Zhaoyue Wu defended a PhD thesis on efficient hyperspectral image processing and anomaly detection at the University of Extremadura, supervised by Juan M. Haut. The defence is listed in the University’s academic profile.
Sep 01, 2024 OASIS, a research project on sustainable AI for hyperspectral remote sensing, began on 1 September 2024. Project PID2023-149880NA-I00 is funded by MICIU/AEI and FEDER, EU, and runs until 31 December 2027, as detailed in the University of Extremadura’s project documentation.
Sep 01, 2023 RAT_EOS_PC began on 1 September 2023, developing early-warning networks for climate risks using Earth observation. The project is co-funded by the EU through Interreg Spain–Portugal (POCTEP) and runs until 31 December 2026. See the project description.
Jan 28, 2022 Juan M. Haut received the University of Extremadura Research Excellence Award for young researchers in the technical field, from the 2021 awards round. The award ceremony took place on 28 January 2022.
